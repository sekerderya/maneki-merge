Maneki Merge privacy policy

Effective 17 October 2026

Maneki Merge is a lucky-cat merge game made by sekerderya. You can play it as a web app (in a browser, or added to your home screen) or as the Maneki Merge app from Google Play. This policy explains what happens to your information in each. The short version: the game asks before it sends anonymous play statistics and crash reports, and sends nothing if you say no; only the Google Play app shows ads, from Google AdMob, and only while your phone is online.

What the game collects: play statistics, only if you say yes

The first time the game starts it asks whether you want to share anonymous play statistics and crash reports. Nothing is sent before you answer, and nothing at all if you say no. You can change your answer at any time in the game's Settings, under Play statistics; once it is off, nothing more is sent.

If you say yes, the game reports how it is played and what goes wrong: when runs and stages start and end, scores, play times, the coins earned and spent, the upgrades and blessings chosen, the ad offers you accept or decline, the frame rate, the screens you open, and error and crash reports. With them, the statistics services receive a random identifier created on your device for these statistics, your device's model, operating system and browser or app version, the type of connection, and your IP address, from which an approximate location (the country) is told. The game never sends your name, email, contacts, photos, your advertising ID or anything you type; there are no accounts.

The developer uses the statistics only to make the game better: to find stages that are too hard, where players stop, and what breaks. They are never sold, and never used for ads. The services that receive them:

  • GameAnalytics (the web app and the Google Play app): gameplay statistics. See the GameAnalytics Privacy Policy.
  • Google Firebase Analytics (the web app and the Google Play app): how often and how long the game is played. On the web it stores its identifier in a cookie of the game's own. See Privacy and Security in Firebase and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics (the Google Play app): crash and error reports.

They keep the statistics for a limited time under their own policies. To have the statistics sent from your device deleted, contact the developer (below) and turn Play statistics off.

The web app: no ads

The web app shows no ads. Apart from the play statistics above, which it sends only with your yes, nothing about you leaves your device.

The Google Play app: ads from Google AdMob

The Android app can offer optional ads that you choose to watch for a reward in the game. They come from Google AdMob, Google's ad service. They only load while your phone has an internet connection; offline the app shows no ads and the game plays as usual.

When an ad loads or shows, Google receives information from your phone, as Google's policy for apps that use its services describes: your device's advertising ID, its IP address (from which an approximate location can be told), information about the device and the app, how you interact with the ads, and diagnostic data. Google uses it to show, measure and protect ads, and, if you allow it, to personalise them. The developer never sees this information. See the Google Privacy Policy.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before ads can use your data, through Google's consent form, the first time it starts with an internet connection. You can change your answer at any time in the game's Settings, under Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

What stays on your device

Your progress (coins, upgrades, best stage and score, a run in progress, your own statistics) and your settings (sound, haptics, reduce motion, day and night, your answer about play statistics) are saved in the app's or your browser's storage on your own device. The save itself is never sent to anyone. Uninstalling the app or clearing the site's data in your browser deletes it for good.

Downloading the game

The web app's files and its updates come from GitHub Pages (sekerderya.github.io). Like any web server, GitHub receives your device's IP address when it sends the files and may keep it in its logs for security; the developer never sees it. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement. After the first launch the web app plays offline. The Google Play app carries the whole game inside it and gets its updates from Google Play.

Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over. The ads in the Google Play app are limited to content suitable for teens with parental guidance (rated PG). The web app shows no ads, and the play statistics are only sent with a yes and hold no name or contact details.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the game: open an issue at github.com/sekerderya/maneki-merge/issues, or use the developer email on the game's Google Play page.
