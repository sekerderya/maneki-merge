Maneki Merge privacy policy

Effective 15 October 2026

Maneki Merge is a lucky-cat merge game made by sekerderya. You can play it as a web app (in a browser, or added to your home screen) or as the Maneki Merge app from Google Play. This policy explains what happens to your information in each. The short version: the game itself collects nothing; only the Google Play app shows ads, from Google AdMob, and only while your phone is online.

What the game collects

Nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking in the game. It doesn't ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.

The web app: no ads, no data

The web app shows no ads and uses no third-party services. Nothing about you leaves your device.

The Google Play app: ads from Google AdMob

The Android app can offer optional ads that you choose to watch for a reward in the game. They come from Google AdMob, Google's ad service. They only load while your phone has an internet connection; offline the app shows no ads and the game plays as usual.

When an ad loads or shows, Google receives information from your phone, as Google's policy for apps that use its services describes: your device's advertising ID, its IP address (from which an approximate location can be told), information about the device and the app, how you interact with the ads, and diagnostic data. Google uses it to show, measure and protect ads, and, if you allow it, to personalise them. The developer never sees this information. See the Google Privacy Policy.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before ads can use your data, through Google's consent form, the first time it starts with an internet connection. You can change your answer at any time in the game's Settings, under Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

What stays on your device

Your progress (coins, upgrades, best stage and score, a run in progress, statistics) and your settings (sound, haptics, reduce motion, day and night) are saved in the app's or your browser's storage on your own device. They are never sent to anyone. Uninstalling the app or clearing the site's data in your browser deletes them for good.

Downloading the game

The web app's files and its updates come from GitHub Pages (sekerderya.github.io). Like any web server, GitHub receives your device's IP address when it sends the files and may keep it in its logs for security; the developer never sees it. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement. After the first launch the web app plays offline. The Google Play app carries the whole game inside it and gets its updates from Google Play.

Children

The Google Play app is meant for players aged 13 and over, and the ads in it are limited to content suitable for teens with parental guidance (rated PG). The web app shows no ads and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the game: open an issue at github.com/sekerderya/maneki-merge/issues, or use the developer email on the game's Google Play page.
