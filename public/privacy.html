Maneki Merge privacy policy

Effective 13 October 2026

Maneki Merge is a lucky-cat merge game made by sekerderya. It runs on your phone as a web app or as the Maneki Merge app from Google Play. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play. The short version: the game collects nothing.

What the game collects

Nothing. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party services in the game. It doesn't ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and the Android app asks for no permissions.

What stays on your device

Your progress (coins, upgrades, best stage and score, a run in progress, statistics) and your settings (sound, haptics, reduce motion) are saved in your browser's storage on your own device. They are never sent to anyone. Uninstalling the app or clearing the site's data in your browser deletes them for good.

Downloading the game

The game's files and its updates come from GitHub Pages (sekerderya.github.io). Like any web server, GitHub receives your device's IP address when it sends the files and may keep it in its logs for security; the developer never sees it. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement. After the first launch the game plays offline. The Android app opens the game in your phone's browser engine (usually Chrome), whose own privacy policy covers the browser.

Children

The game is for everyone and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the game: open an issue at github.com/sekerderya/maneki-merge/issues, or use the developer email on the game's Google Play page.
